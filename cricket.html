Cricket is a bat-and-ball team sport where two sides of eleven players take turns batting and fielding to score the most runsThe Basics of PlayThe Field: Played on a large oval grass field with a 22-yard rectangular pitch in the center.The Wicket: Consists of three vertical wooden posts (stumps) and two small horizontal pieces (bails) on top, placed at each end of the pitch.Scoring: Batters score runs by hitting the ball and running between the wickets, hitting a boundary along the ground for four runs, or clearing the boundary in the air for six runs.
